## SLIDE 3: THE INNOVATION — AGENTIC FUEL

### The Billing Engine That Hedges Against Currency Devaluation

- **1 Credit = $0.10 USD** — pegged via real-time FX rates
- **Live FX Hedging** — rates fetched every 15 minutes for NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, UGX, TZS
- **Protection** — clients pay in local currency but credits are valued in USD
- **Revenue certainty** — Dutchkem captures margin regardless of Naira/Cedi devaluation

**This is not just billing — it's a currency risk management layer.**
