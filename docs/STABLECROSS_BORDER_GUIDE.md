## How It Works

### Step 1: Fund Your Transfer
- Pay via card (Visa/Mastercard) or bank transfer
- Supported currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD

### Step 2: Stablecoin Conversion
- Your funds are converted to USDT or USDC on-chain
- Smart contract escrow holds funds securely
- No counterparty risk — funds are verifiably locked

### Step 3: Last-Mile Delivery
- **Mobile Money:** M-Pesa (Kenya), MoMo (Ghana/Nigeria), Airtel Money
- **Bank Transfer:** Direct to any Nigerian, Ghanaian, Kenyan bank account
- **Cash Pickup:** Partner agent locations across Africa

### Step 4: Confirmation
- Real-time tracking via WhatsApp/Telegram bot
- SMS confirmation to sender and receiver
- Transaction hash provided for blockchain verification
